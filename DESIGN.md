## Overview

kairo uses a calm, operational dashboard aesthetic: neutral surfaces, restrained shadows, and confident emerald accents for primary actions. The interface should feel fast, utilitarian, and quietly premium, with information density tuned for market operations rather than marketing flair.

## Colors

- **Primary:** Emerald green for primary actions, active states, and success indicators.
- **Secondary:** Teal for supporting interactive elements and informational emphasis.
- **Tertiary:** Amber-brown for warnings, recovery states, and cautionary highlights.
- **Neutral:** Zinc/slate neutrals for page backgrounds, borders, and body text hierarchy.
